Build a production-ready Arabic-first real estate consultancy SaaS platform from scratch with the following specifications:

Core Concept: Passive real estate consultancy and market intelligence. Clients provide their property interests and get AI-driven market insights, risk analysis, and personalized recommendations. The system captures all key market data (via Gemini AI JSON or manual input) and stores it for analytics.

Pricing Model:

Initial consultation fee: 200 EGP to filter serious clients.

Commission: 2% on any deal completed through the platform.

System should allow tracking payments and commissions automatically.

Neuro-Marketing Features:

Behavioral tracking (scroll depth, clicks, time-on-page, hesitation signals) to detect engagement and seriousness.

Trust & authority design: deep blue + gold + white color palette, copywriting to reinforce expertise.

Personalization: Recommendations based on client profile, preferences, risk tolerance.

Scarcity & urgency cues: Limited time opportunities, real-time alerts.

Reward/referral system for clients who bring new leads.

Platform Features:

Role-based Authentication: Admin, Client, Developer using JWT/Replit Auth.

Admin Dashboard: Upload and parse Gemini AI JSON market data, monitor behavioral analytics, developer trust scores, market trends, and client activity.

Client Interface: Interactive consultation forms, AI-assisted buyer profile builder, contract/document upload, market insights, AI-driven recommendations.

Developer Dashboard: Manage property info, track client leads, view trust scores and complaint history.

Smart Matching Engine: Match clients to properties and developers using behavioral data, preferences, risk tolerance, and budget.

Behavioral Micro-Tracking: Track all interactions in GDPR-compliant way.

Contract & Risk Analysis: Upload contracts, parse PDF/Word, detect risks and clauses.

Notifications: Alerts for market changes, opportunities, consultation reminders.

Analytics: Client journey, interaction heatmaps, conversion funnels, trust metrics.

Security: Rate limiting, input validation, secure file handling.

UI/UX:

Fully Arabic-first RTL design, responsive mobile layout, bilingual support (Arabic/English).

Dashboard visuals to convey authority, trust, and high value.

Goal: Maximize client retention, seriousness, and conversion, capturing the entire consultation journey, and generating revenue from consultation fees + successful deal commission
